Assets

Configuration Managed Assets Overview​

Asset records form the basis of the as built and as maintained (operational) configuration data.
They are used to manage and maintain the configuration of the asset over the course of its operational life time.

Configuration managed assets are distinguished from standard Maximo (non-CM) assets by their association with a CM Item (and by extension a Model).

CM assets also behave and are treated differently to non-CM assets, for example:

  • Configuration managed assets are validated by the Build Data Interepeter (BDI) whereas non-CM Assets are not.
  • Continuous type meter readings are recorded and calculated differently for CM Assets (covered in detail in the Meters section of this documentation).
  • PM records for CM Assets are treated differentlty to those of non-CM assets.

End Items and Non-End Items​

Configuration managed assets can be further categorised as End Items or Non-End Items. The key distinction is that an end item asset is one created from the top-level postion within a model. This could be a parent model, such as an aicraft or train set, or an "installable" (sometimes referred to as a sub model) representing a major assembly that could be used in multiple positions eg. an engine or train car.

Conversely a non-end item is one created from a child build position in a model.

Additional ASSET Object Attributes:​

CM Managed Assets have a number of other additional data attributes to that of standard Maximo Assets. The primary ones of interest are detailed below:

Attribute NameTypeEnd-Item / CM AssetsDescription
PLUSACATIDBIGINTAllThe unique id of the associated CM Item (PLUSACACAT).
PLUSAMODELIDBIGINTEnd-ItemThe unique id of the associated Model (PLUSACMODEL).
PLUSACONFIGIDBIGINTEnd-ItemThe unique id of the associated Variant (Primary) Configuration (PLUSACMCONFIG).
PLUSAREVISIONIDBIGINTEnd-ItemThe unique id of the Revision of the Model applied to the asset (PLUSACMREVISION).
PLUSAREGUPPEREnd-ItemThe current Registration value for the asset.
PLUSAMNFCTRDATEDATETIMEAllThe date of manufacture of the asset.
PLUSARCPTDATEDATETIMEAllthe date of receipt of the asset into the system.

Assets with an associated Model (ASSET.PLUSAMODELID IS NOT NULL) are considered by the system to be End Items.
